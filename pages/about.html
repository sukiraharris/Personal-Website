Meet Me

I'm a second-year Computer Science student at Northeastern University with a minor in Behavioural Neuroscience. I'm passionate about the intersection of technology and health, and I'm motivated by building solutions that are both innovative and human-centered.

I've always loved creating, whether through fashion, which has been a core form of self-expression for me, or through reading and writing, which I turn to constantly for inspiration and reflection.

Movement and sport have shaped who I am just as much as academics. From rugby and endurance sports like running and cycling to dance, hiking, and calisthenics, I've grown up valuing challenge, discipline, and physical expression. I try to carry that same mindset into everything I do: pushing myself, staying curious, and giving 100%.
