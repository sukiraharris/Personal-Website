About me

Meet Me

I’m a third-year Computer Science student at Northeastern University, concentrating in Artificial Intelligence and minoring in Neuroscience. I’m interested in the intersection of computation, biology, and behavior, particularly how technology can help us understand and interact with complex living systems.

My work has taken me across several areas of research and technology. In Harvard’s Engert Lab, I worked with animal behavior and computer vision, and I now explore animal-computer interaction alongside software and AI projects. I’m particularly interested in neurotechnology, computational neuroscience, health technology, and human-centered AI. I’m drawn to technical problems that require an understanding of more than just the technology itself, especially when biology, behavior, or human experience are part of the problem.

Outside of computer science, creativity is a large part of my life. Fashion has always been one of my main forms of self-expression, and as Creative Director of Avenue, I work across creative direction, visual storytelling, and leadership. I also spend a lot of time reading and writing, which gives me another way to explore ideas that interest me beyond technical work.

Sport has shaped the way I approach challenges as well. I run marathons and cycle, and over the years I’ve also been involved in rugby, dance, hiking, and calisthenics. Across all of these interests, I’m motivated by curiosity and the challenge of learning something deeply. I enjoy moving between disciplines, finding connections between seemingly unrelated ideas, and taking on work that pushes me beyond what I already know.
